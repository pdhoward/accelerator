### Evaluation

- **AT&T Extra 2.0** is the best match for what you want. During the 36-month credits it is substantially cheaper (~$10–40/month savings depending on exact taxes). It is better than your Verizon plan on data priority (100 GB of high-speed before any potential slowdown vs. deprioritized from the start) and adds a generous 50 GB hotspot that Verizon Welcome completely lacks. Mexico/Canada and international texting are comparable or better, and ActiveArmor gives solid spam/security features. After the promo ends it is still competitive with (or close to) your current $140 all-in, while remaining the stronger plan.

- **AT&T Value 2.0** is the cheapest by a wide margin and still beats Verizon on having *some* hotspot and a clearer (if small) high-speed allotment. However, 5 GB of priority data is quite limited for most people who do any streaming, maps, social media, or light work away from Wi-Fi. Only choose this if both lines are very light cellular users who stay mostly on Wi-Fi.

- **AT&T Premium 2.0** is superior in every major category (true unlimited priority data, 100 GB hotspot, 4K streaming, expanded Latin America coverage). It is *not* “much lower cost”—it matches your current price during the promo and becomes more expensive afterward. Skip it unless you need the premium features or travel a lot to Latin America.

### Additional notes
Because Verizon service is poor where you live and you already have AT&T internet, switching makes practical sense for coverage. Confirm AT&T signal strength at home, work, and your regular routes (coverage maps or a short trial if available). You may also be able to stack a mobile + home discount on top of the quoted mobile rates—ask the rep, as your quotes appear to be mobility-only.

The port-in and AutoPay discounts are critical to the quoted prices; confirm they apply fully and that any activation fees are waived or minimized. Taxes vary by ZIP code, so request a final taxes-and-fees-included estimate from AT&T before committing.

**Bottom line:** Go with **Extra 2.0**. It delivers better real-world performance and features than your current Verizon Unlimited Welcome at a lower (or at worst comparable) monthly cost, especially for the first three years.
